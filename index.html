About us

A local building and installation company

ARY BUILDERS LTD is a private limited company based in Chadderton, Oldham, in Greater Manchester. We carry out general building work, install thermal and sound insulation, and fit doors and other fixed fittings in buildings.

The company was incorporated in England and Wales on 6 March 2025 under company number 16299314. It is registered under SIC code 43290, “other construction installation”, the official classification for installation work carried out in buildings.

We are an independent business, owned and run directly by our director. There is no parent company or group behind us.

Services

What we do

Our work falls into three general areas. Every job is different, so please get in touch with a short description of what you need.

General building work

Building tasks for homes and small commercial properties. Write to us with a description of the job and we will reply to discuss it.

  • Homes
  • Small commercial properties
  • General building tasks

Insulation installation

Installation of thermal and sound insulation in existing and new buildings, to help keep heat in and reduce noise passing between rooms and from outside.

  • Thermal insulation
  • Sound insulation
  • Existing and new buildings

Doors and building fittings

Fitting doors and other fixed installations in buildings, carried out as a job on its own or as part of wider building work.

  • Door installation
  • Other fixed building fittings
  • Standalone or as part of a larger job
